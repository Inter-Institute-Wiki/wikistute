# Contribute

Team Wikistute appreciates meaningful contributions to the project and its contents in any form via [GitHub](https://github.com/Inter-Institute-Wiki/wikistute)!

Alternately, you can mail us your content writeup [interinstitutewiki@gmail.com](mailto:interinstitutewiki@gmail.com). The project's maintainers would review your content and then add it to the website with appropriate credits.
